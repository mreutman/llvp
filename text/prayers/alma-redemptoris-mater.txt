# Loving Mother of the Redeemer, who remains the accessible gateway of heaven and star of the sea, hasten to aid the falling people, who strive to rise: you who gave birth, while nature marveled, to your own holy Creator, a Virgin before and after, receiving that 'Ave' from the mouth of Gabriel, have mercy on sinners.
Àlma Redemptóris Mater, qvae pèrvia ċoeli porta mànes et stella maris, succùrre cadènti, sùrġere qvi curat, pòpulo: tu qvae ġenuìsti, natúra mirànte, tuum sânctum ġenitórem, Virǧo prius ac postèrius, Gabriélis ab ore sumens illud ave, peccatórum miserére.
